About Us

Fueling the Curiosity of Makers

Notion VIT Bhopal is the campus productivity & tech community dedicated to fostering learning, building real projects, and creating a space where curious students turn ideas into outcomes.

  • Weekly hands-on workshops & sessions
  • Mentorship from industry practitioners
  • Hackathons, bootcamps & competitions
  • Notion templates & productivity resources

Ideate

Turn vague curiosity into scoped, actionable project ideas through structured sessions.

Build

Ship real projects with AI tools, dev stacks, and hands-on mentorship.

Connect

Network with industry speakers, alumni, and a campus community of builders.

Grow

Level up through masterclasses, certifications, and cross-domain exposure.
